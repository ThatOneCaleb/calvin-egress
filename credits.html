Credits & references

The shoulders this stands on.

Calvin Egress draws on established pedestrian science and fire-safety engineering, open geographic data, and open-source graphics.

01  Science

  • Weidmann — pedestrian fundamental diagram (density → walking speed).
  • Fruin — pedestrian level-of-service and free-flow walking speeds.
  • SFPE Handbook — pre-movement time distributions and the ASET/RSET framework.
  • Purser (FED) — fractional effective dose model for smoke tenability.
  • Jin — visibility and walking speed in smoke; wrong-turn behavior.
  • RiMEA — standard verification and validation test cases for evacuation models.

02  Data & technology

  • OpenStreetMap — building footprints for the Calvin campus. © OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Hekman Library floor plans — digitized to the 1 m model grid.
  • Three.js — WebGL rendering of the real-time 3D view.
  • Plain JavaScript engine — a dependency-free, Node-testable simulation core that runs entirely in the browser.
  • Type — Archivo, Plus Jakarta Sans, and JetBrains Mono (Google Fonts).

03  Project

Built & maintained by Caleb Steele

For Calvin University safety planning. Engine, geometry pipeline, and this site are developed as one evolving project. For collaboration, data contributions (finer floor plans especially), or questions about the model, reach out through the Calvin University research context under which it's developed.
